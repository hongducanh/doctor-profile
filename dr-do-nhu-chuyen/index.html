FAQs

Questions patients ask

Can I complete implant treatment in one trip?

Usually it takes two trips. On the first trip (about 5–7 days) Dr. Chris places the implants and, for All-on-4 or All-on-6 where bone allows, fits a fixed temporary bridge. After the implants heal, typically 3–6 months, a second trip of a few days fits the final bridge or crowns. Cases that need bone grafting can take longer; Dr. Chris confirms the plan after your scan.

How long do dental implants last?

Implants are designed as a long-term solution. With good home care and regular check-ups many last 15–20 years or longer. The crowns or bridge on top may need maintenance or replacement over time.

Can I eat normally after full-mouth implant treatment?

Yes, once healing is complete and your final restoration is fitted. Straight after surgery we give you a short soft-food guide to protect the healing tissue.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

Both replace a full arch of teeth with a fixed bridge. All-on-6 uses six implants instead of four, which gives extra support and can suit patients with lower bone density or a stronger bite.

How is the cost decided?

You receive a written quote after the examination and scan, before any treatment starts. Items that depend on your scan, such as bone grafting, sinus lift or IV sedation, are quoted as a range so there are no surprises.
