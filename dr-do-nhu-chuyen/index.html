Frequently asked questions

1Can I complete my implant treatment in one trip?+

Many cases, including All-on-4 and All-on-6, can be completed within a single 5-7 day visit. More complex cases involving grafting may need a short second trip for final restorations.

2How long do dental implants last?+

With proper care and regular check-ups, implants can last 15-20 years or longer. The surrounding restoration may need replacement over time, but the implant itself is designed to be a long-term solution.

3Can I eat normally after full mouth implant treatment?+

Yes. Once healing is complete and your final restoration is fitted, you can eat a normal diet. We provide a short-term soft food guide immediately after surgery to protect healing tissue.

4What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?+

Both replace a full arch of teeth with a fixed bridge. All-on-6 uses six implants instead of four, offering additional support that can suit patients with lower bone density or higher bite force.

5How much can I save on full-mouth implants in Vietnam?+

International patients typically save 50-70% compared to costs in the US, UK, Australia or New Zealand, without compromising on materials or technique. We provide a fixed quote after your consultation.
